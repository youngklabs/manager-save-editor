Privacy Policy

How Manager Save Editor handles files and information.

한국어English
Effective date: November 18, 2026
Game files are processed on your device. The app does not collect personal information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers, or transmit it to the developer.

2. Files and device storage

  • The app reads game save files you select and writes changes to those files when you choose to save.
  • Copies of recently opened files and settings such as language and theme are kept in app-private storage. This app-private data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • If you optionally grant Shizuku permission, the app can read game graphics files and modify them at your request. It may also clear related caches when graphics are deleted.
  • Game files are processed on your device and are not sent to a developer server or shared with third parties.

3. Advertising and analytics

The app contains no ads, analytics tools or tracking tools.

4. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from users, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
